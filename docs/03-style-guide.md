Style Guide

Version: 1.0

Purpose

This document defines the visual identity and typographical standards for all Sacrae Preces publications.

Every volume in the series should present a consistent appearance, ensuring that readers immediately recognize the Sacrae Preces identity regardless of the subject or volume number.

Design Philosophy

The visual design of Sacrae Preces should reflect the same principles as its content.

Beauty should serve prayer.
Simplicity is preferable to excess.
White space is intentional.
Typography should encourage slow, reflective reading.
Every design choice should support the reader's encounter with God.

Borders and Ornaments

Borders should be subtle.

Use only where they enhance the page.

Preferred ornament style:

Medieval
Romanesque
Early Gothic

Avoid elaborate Victorian decoration.

Images

Images should:

Support the devotional content.
Be historically appropriate.
Be high resolution.
Maintain a consistent artistic style.

Avoid decorative images without purpose.

Tables

Vocabulary tables should use a consistent three-column layout:

Latin
English
Notes

Minimal borders.

No shading unless required.

Quotations

Scripture and quotations should be visually distinct from the main body text.

Long quotations should be indented.

White Space

Do not attempt to fill every page.

A devotional book should feel calm and unhurried.

White space is part of the design.

Consistency Checklist

Before publication, verify:

Correct fonts.
Correct paragraph styles.
Consistent spacing.
Correct running headers.
Correct page numbering.
Images correctly aligned.
Tables follow house style.
Colours remain within the approved palette.
No manual formatting overrides the defined styles.
