Typography Guide

Version: 1.0

Purpose

This document defines the typographical standards for all Sacrae Preces publications.

Typography is one of the primary ways readers experience the calm, beauty, and reverence of the series. Consistency is therefore essential.

Design Philosophy

Typography should never distract from prayer.

The page should feel peaceful, spacious, and inviting.

Readers should almost forget they are looking at typography.

Capitalization

Chapter titles should use Title Case.

Prayer text should preserve the capitalization found in the approved liturgical text.

Italics

Use italics for:

Latin prayers
Scripture quotations
Foreign words on first use
Book titles

Do not overuse italics for emphasis.

Bold

Use bold sparingly.

Reserved primarily for headings.

Drop Caps

Each chapter opens with an illuminated initial.

The remainder of the chapter uses standard typography.

Manual Formatting

Avoid manual formatting whenever possible.

All formatting should be applied using predefined paragraph styles.

If a formatting change is required, update the style rather than the individual paragraph.

Future Revisions

This guide may evolve as additional volumes are published, but changes should preserve the established identity of the Sacrae Preces series.
