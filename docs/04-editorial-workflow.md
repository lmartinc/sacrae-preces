Editorial Workflow

Version: 1.0

Purpose

This document defines the editorial process for developing every chapter in the Sacrae Preces series. Following a consistent workflow ensures theological accuracy, editorial consistency, and a high standard of quality across all volumes.

Editorial Workflow
Stage 1 – Research

Collect source material from:

Sacred Scripture
Catechism of the Catholic Church
Official liturgical texts
Church Fathers
Doctors of the Church
Papal documents (where appropriate)
Reliable historical and theological references

Record sources before drafting begins.

Stage 2 – Planning

Prepare the chapter outline.

Define:

Prayer
Scripture references
Historical background
Reflection themes
Vocabulary
Supporting artwork
Stage 3 – First Draft

Write the complete chapter without focusing on perfection.

Prioritize:

Clarity
Accuracy
Natural flow
Stage 4 – Theological Review

Confirm that:

Catholic doctrine is presented faithfully.
Scripture is correctly referenced.
Historical statements are supported.
Reflections are clearly distinguished from doctrine.
Stage 5 – Editorial Review

Review for:

Grammar
Style
Consistency
Readability
Formatting
Stage 6 – Layout

Apply the official Sacrae Preces template.

Confirm:

Styles
Images
Tables
Headers
Page numbering
Stage 7 – Proofreading

Perform a final page-by-page review.

Look specifically for:

Typographical errors
Formatting inconsistencies
Incorrect references
Broken page layouts
Stage 8 – Publication Approval

The chapter is approved only when all editorial, theological, and formatting checks have been completed.

Editorial Checklist
Research complete
Scripture verified
Catechism references verified
Reflection reviewed
Images approved
Layout complete
Proofread complete
Ready for publication
