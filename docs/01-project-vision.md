Sacrae Preces exists to help people encounter God more deeply through the timeless prayers of the Catholic Church.

Its purpose is to make the Church's rich prayer tradition accessible to all—whether lifelong Catholics, those preparing to enter the Church, families praying together, or anyone seeking to understand the beauty and history of Catholic prayer.

Each prayer is presented in both English and Ecclesiastical Latin, allowing readers to appreciate the meaning of the words while becoming familiar with the language that has shaped centuries of Christian worship.

Beyond the prayers themselves, Sacrae Preces seeks to deepen understanding by providing:

The prayer in English and Latin, presented side by side.
Its foundation in Sacred Scripture.
Historical background and its place in the life of the Church.
An explanation of its meaning and, where appropriate, the significance of each petition.
Key Latin words and phrases to encourage a gradual familiarity with Ecclesiastical Latin.
Guidance on when and how the prayer is traditionally used.
Short devotional reflections intended to encourage prayer and contemplation.

Sacrae Preces is not intended to replace Sacred Scripture, the Sacred Liturgy, or the official teaching of the Catholic Church. Rather, it is offered as a faithful companion to them, helping readers to pray with greater understanding, reverence, and confidence.

Every page is written according to three guiding principles:

Accuracy — faithfully presenting the teaching and tradition of the Catholic Church.
Reverence — reflecting the dignity and beauty of Christian prayer.
Accessibility — making the treasures of the Church understandable and approachable for readers of all ages and backgrounds.

Our hope is that each volume of Sacrae Preces will not merely be read, but prayed, studied, and returned to throughout a lifetime, becoming a trusted companion in the spiritual journey of those who seek a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ.

Beauty should serve prayer.
